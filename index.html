Behind The Lens

Hi, I'm Julian Vance

Based in Vancouver but available worldwide, I am a fine-art photographer dedicated to capturing the raw, unscripted beauty of human connection and natural environments.

Over the last decade, my work has been featured in National Geographic, Vogue Editorial, and numerous international exhibitions. My philosophy is grounded in minimalism—eliminating visual noise to let authentic emotion radiate.
